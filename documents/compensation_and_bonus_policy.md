# Compensation, Bonus, and Retirement Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HR-2025-004  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Version:** 3.1  
**Owner:** Global Compensation & Executive Committee  

---

## 1. Annual Compensation Review Cycle
- **Timeline:** The annual merit and compensation review begins in January, with adjustments taking effect on April 1 payroll.
- **Rating Scales:** Performance is calibrated across five tiers: 1 (Needs Improvement), 2 (Partially Meets), 3 (Consistently Meets), 4 (Exceeds Expectations), 5 (Exceptional Impact).
- **Merit Increases:** Merit increases are determined by a combination of performance rating and market compa-ratio (position against market median).

## 2. Annual Incentive Performance Bonus
- **Eligibility:** Full-time regular employees who joined on or before September 30 of the performance year are eligible for a pro-rated or full annual bonus.
- **Bonus Targets by Job Band:**
  - Individual Contributors (Band 1 - Band 2): 10% of base annual salary.
  - Senior & Lead Professionals (Band 3): 15% of base annual salary.
  - Managers and Principal Roles (Band 4): 20% of base annual salary.
  - Directors and Executives (Band 5+): 25% - 35% of base annual salary.
- **Multiplier Components:** Payout is weighted 60% on company financial targets and 40% on individual OKR achievements.

## 3. 401(k) / Pension Retirement Plan Matching
- **Company Match Formula:** The company matches 100% of the employee's contribution on the first 4% of eligible salary, plus 50% on the next 2% of salary (maximum total company match of 5.0%).
- **Vesting Schedule:** Employee contributions are immediately 100% vested. Company matching contributions vest on a 2-year cliff (100% vested after 24 months of continuous service).

## 4. Spot Recognition and Peer Bonuses
- **Spot Excellence Award:** Managers may nominate employees for immediate cash awards ranging between \$250 and \$1,000 for exceptional project delivery or crisis resolution.
- **Peer-to-Peer Kudos:** Employees receive 500 Kudos points monthly (\$50 value) to award to colleagues who embody core company values.
