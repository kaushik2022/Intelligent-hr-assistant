# Enterprise Leave and Attendance Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HR-2025-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Owner:** Global Human Resources Department  

---

## 1. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
- **Accrual Rate:** Full-time employees accrue 20 working days of paid time off per calendar year, accruing at 1.67 days per month worked.
- **Eligibility:** PTO begins accruing on the first day of employment and is available for use after the 30-day onboarding period.
- **Rollover & Expiration:** A maximum of 5 unused PTO days can be rolled over into the following calendar year. Any rolled-over days must be utilized by March 31 (Q1 close), after which they will lapse without cash encashment.
- **Advance Notice:** Requests for PTO of 3 consecutive days or longer must be submitted via the HR Portal at least 10 business days in advance.

## 2. Sick and Casual Medical Leave
- **Entitlement:** All employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, credited upfront on January 1.
- **Certification Requirement:** For absences lasting up to 2 consecutive business days, no formal medical certificate is required; an email notification to the manager by 9:00 AM local time is sufficient.
- **Extended Illness:** Absences of 3 or more consecutive days require a signed doctor's certificate uploaded to the HR Portal upon return.
- **Conversion:** Unused sick days do not roll over to the next year and cannot be cashed out upon separation.

## 3. Parental Leave Policy
- **Primary Caregiver (Maternity Leave):** Eligible employees giving birth or adopting a child under 1 year of age receive 26 consecutive weeks of 100% paid leave.
- **Secondary Caregiver (Paternity Leave):** Eligible non-birthing partners receive 4 weeks of fully paid leave, which may be taken consecutively or in two 2-week blocks within the child's first 12 months.
- **Transition Support:** Returning parents are eligible for a 4-week "Gradual Return" program (working 80% hours at 100% compensation).

## 4. Bereavement and Compassionate Leave
- **Immediate Family:** Up to 5 consecutive business days of fully paid leave are granted in the event of the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, partner, child, parent, sibling).
- **Extended Family:** Up to 3 business days for extended family (grandparents, in-laws, grandchildren).
- **Additional Unpaid Days:** Employees may request up to 5 additional unpaid days subject to manager approval.

## 5. Floating Holidays and Statutory Days
- **Company Holidays:** 10 mandatory paid public holidays declared annually by regional HR.
- **Floating Holidays:** Every employee receives 2 additional floating holidays per calendar year to observe religious, cultural, or personal events not covered on the statutory calendar.
