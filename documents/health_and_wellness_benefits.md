# Health, Medical, and Wellness Benefits Guide

**Document ID:** POL-HR-2025-003  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Version:** 4.0  
**Owner:** Total Rewards & Benefits Committee  

---

## 1. Comprehensive Medical, Dental, and Vision Insurance
- **Coverage Tiers:** The company offers three plan options administered through Blue Cross: Basic Silver, Preferred Gold (Default), and Comprehensive Platinum.
- **Premium Subsidies:** The company covers 90% of employee premium contributions and 75% of dependent/family coverage premiums.
- **Dental Benefits:** Includes 100% preventative care coverage (two routine cleanings and checkups per year) and up to \$2,000 annual maximum for restorative care.
- **Vision Care:** Covers one comprehensive eye exam every 12 months with a \$10 co-pay, plus a \$250 annual credit toward prescription eyeglasses or contact lenses.

## 2. Mental Health and Emotional Well-being (EAP)
- **Employee Assistance Program:** Access to Modern Health EAP offering 8 confidential, company-paid therapy sessions per year per household member.
- **Crisis Hotlines:** 24/7 complimentary crisis helpline with licensed clinical psychologists and social workers.
- **Wellness Days:** Two global "Recharge Days" are scheduled annually where company operations pause for mental well-being.

## 3. Annual Fitness and Wellness Allowance
- **Subsidy Amount:** Up to \$500 per employee per calendar year.
- **Eligible Expenses:** Gym memberships, yoga studio passes, fitness wearables (Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin), meditation app subscriptions (Headspace, Calm), and ergonomic running shoes.
- **Claim Window:** Receipts must be submitted by December 15 of the benefit year.

## 4. Annual Executive Preventive Health Screening
- **Preventive Care Day:** Employees are granted 1 paid half-day off to complete an annual health screening.
- **Reimbursement:** 100% coverage for routine biometric screenings, blood panels, lipid profiles, and cardiovascular assessments with in-network providers.
