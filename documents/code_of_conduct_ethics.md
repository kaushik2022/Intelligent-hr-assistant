# Code of Conduct, Ethics, and Dispute Resolution

**Document ID:** POL-HR-2025-005  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Version:** 5.0  
**Owner:** Legal & Global People Compliance  

---

## 1. Equal Opportunity and Anti-Harassment Standards
- **Zero-Tolerance Stance:** The company maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding discrimination, harassment, bullying, or microaggressions based on race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, religion, or age.
- **Reporting Channels:** Any violation may be reported directly to HR, any People Manager, or anonymously through the 24/7 Global Ethics Hotline.
- **Non-Retaliation Guarantee:** Any form of retaliation against an employee who reports a concern or participates in an investigation in good faith is strictly illegal and grounds for immediate dismissal.

## 2. Conflicts of Interest and Outside Employment
- **Moonlighting & Secondary Work:** Outside consulting, advisory roles, or secondary employment must be formally disclosed and approved by the Legal & HR Compliance panel via the Conflict of Interest Disclosure portal.
- **Competitor Restrictions:** Engaging in commercial activities with direct competitors or using company intellectual property for private ventures is strictly prohibited.
- **Gifts and Hospitality:** Employees may not accept gifts, meals, or entertainment from vendors or clients exceeding \$100 in value without prior written approval.

## 3. Disciplinary Action Process
- **Step 1: Informal Coaching:** Manager guidance and documented verbal discussion.
- **Step 2: Formal Written Warning:** HR-facilitated Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) or behavioral agreement with a 30-to-60-day review period.
- **Step 3: Final Written Warning:** Issued for repeat infractions or serious policy breaches.
- **Step 4: Termination of Employment:** Triggered by failure to meet PIP standards or immediate termination for gross misconduct (theft, physical violence, fraud, serious data breaches).
