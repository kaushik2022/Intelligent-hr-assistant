# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HR-2025-002  
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2025  
**Version:** 2.4  
**Owner:** Workplace Experience & Global People Team  

---

## 1. Work Model Classifications
- **Remote-First:** Employees hired for designated remote roles work primarily from a home office within their country of contract.
- **Hybrid Arrangement:** Employees assigned to a regional office are expected to work from the office a minimum of 2 days per week (Tuesday and Thursday recommended).
- **Office-Centric:** Roles requiring physical presence (hardware labs, office facilities) report to company premises 4 to 5 days per week.

## 2. Core Working Hours and Availability
- **Synchronous Collaboration Window:** Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone. During this window, employees should be available on Slack and responsive via email.
- **Async Flexibility:** The remaining 3 working hours may be scheduled flexibly before 10:00 AM or after 3:00 PM, provided daily milestones and meeting commitments are met.

## 3. Home Office Equipment Stipend and Subsidies
- **Initial Setup Allowance:** All full-time remote and hybrid employees are eligible for a one-time, non-taxable \$750 home office setup stipend. Approved items include ergonomic chairs, external monitors, standing desks, and noise-canceling headsets.
- **Monthly Connectivity Reimbursement:** Employees may claim up to \$60 per month for high-speed home internet and mobile data via the corporate expense reimbursement portal.
- **Company Hardware:** The IT department provides a standardized laptop, docking hub, and security token. Personal devices may not be used for source code or corporate database access.

## 4. Cybersecurity and Data Privacy for Remote Work
- **Virtual Private Network (VPN):** Connection to the corporate VPN is mandatory whenever accessing internal repositories, SQLite/production databases, or customer PII.
- **Unsecured Networks:** Connecting to open public Wi-Fi networks (cafes, airports, hotels) is strictly prohibited unless routed through the company VPN with DNS leak protection enabled.
- **Screen Privacy:** Laptops must be locked immediately whenever unattended. Privacy screen filters must be applied when working in public shared workspaces.

## 5. Work From Anywhere (WFA) Policy
- **Temporary Relocation:** Employees in good standing may work remotely from another domestic location or pre-approved international country for up to 30 calendar days per fiscal year.
- **Prior Approvals:** A formal WFA request must be lodged with HR and your People Manager at least 3 weeks prior to travel to assess tax and visa compliance.
